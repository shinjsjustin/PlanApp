ShinPlan
ShinPlan is a personal planner made by Justin Shin for his own use. It keeps his projects, to-do lists and a short calendar of the next few days in one place.
If you connect a Google account, ShinPlan reads your Google Calendar so your events show next to your plans, can add the time blocks you plan in ShinPlan to your Google Calendar, and can look up files in your Google Drive that you ask it to read.
ShinPlan is a private tool, not a public service: accounts are created by invitation only.

Privacy Policy
Last updated: [date of deploy]
ShinPlan ("ShinPlan", "we") is a personal planner operated by Justin Shin, an individual, at www.shinplan.com. This policy explains what ShinPlan does with your information, including data from your Google account. ShinPlan is used privately by invitation only.

What we collect
- Account details: your name, username and a hashed password.
- What you put into ShinPlan: projects, to-do lists, calendar days, notes, and any contacts and places you add.
- If you connect Google: your Google email address, and access tokens that let ShinPlan act for you. The tokens are stored encrypted on our server and are never sent to your browser.

How we use Google user data
- Google Calendar: ShinPlan reads the events on your primary calendar for the days shown in your ShinPlan calendar, and saves each event's title, location and times so it can display them. When you export your plan, ShinPlan creates, moves or deletes the events it made for your planned time blocks in your Google Calendar, and keeps a record of which events it made.
- Google Drive (read-only): ShinPlan can list your files and read the text of a document, spreadsheet or text file when you or your assistant ask it to. It does not change or delete anything in your Drive, and it does not store file contents.
- Travel times: when you plan a trip between two places, the addresses are sent to Google's Routes service to estimate drive time.
- AI assistant: Justin's personal AI assistant can read your ShinPlan data and the Google data described above through ShinPlan, to help plan your day. It runs on Anthropic's Claude service, which processes that data to answer your requests.
We use Google user data only to provide these features to you. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers named above as needed to provide the features. ShinPlan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security
Your data is stored on our server. Google tokens are encrypted at rest. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is perfectly secure.

Your choices
- You can disconnect Google at any time in ShinPlan's settings; this deletes the stored tokens. You can also remove ShinPlan's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- You can ask us to delete your account and data by contacting us.

Children
ShinPlan is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes
If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell users before the change takes effect.
